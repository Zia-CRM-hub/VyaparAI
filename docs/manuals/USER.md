# User manual (shop staff)

For Sales, Measurement Specialists, Production Managers, Tailors and Cashiers. Version 0.3.0, 2026-11-24. You only see the screens your role allows; if something is missing, ask your manager.

## Sign in
Open the app, enter your email and password. On a phone you get a bottom bar (Home, Orders, **+ New order**, Customers, Menu); on a computer a side menu. The bell shows alerts.

## The main journey
Customer -> Family member -> Measurement -> Order -> Stitching -> Payment -> Delivery.

## Sales / Measurement Specialist

### Add a customer
**Customers** -> Add. Name and phone are required; adding gender and age group also creates the customer's own profile ("Self") so measurements can be saved straight away.

### Add family members
Open the customer -> **Family** -> add (name, relation, gender, age group). Each person keeps their own measurement history.

### Take or update measurements
Open the person's profile -> Measurements. Choose the garment: the right fields appear for the gender and age group (kids do not see adult-only fields). Enter values and save.
- Nothing changed: the previous measurements are reused.
- Something changed: a new version is saved; older versions stay in the history.

### Create an order (fewest clicks)
1. **+ New order**.
2. Search the customer (or quick-add).
3. Pick the person, then the garment. Latest saved measurements fill in automatically; edit if needed.
4. Set charges, promised date, tailor, notes.
5. Enter any advance and choose the payment method. Save.

Measurements can be changed on an order until cutting starts; after that they are locked for that order.

### Record a payment
Order page -> **Add payment**. You cannot take more than the balance. Pressing twice will not double-charge.

## Production Manager and Tailor
- **Production**: orders by stage. Tailors see only their assigned orders.
- Open an order and press the big action button to move it forward: Order Received -> Measurement -> Cutting -> Stitching -> Trial -> Alteration -> Ready.
- Cutting needs measurements to be attached.
- After a trial, move to Alteration if changes are needed, otherwise Ready.
- If two people press at once, the second sees a message to refresh; nothing is moved twice.

## Delivery (Manager / Production Manager)
**Delivery** lists Ready orders. Mark **Delivered** only when the balance is zero; collect it first with Add payment.

## Cashier
**Payments** lists orders with a balance. Open one, add the payment (cash, UPI, card, etc.), and the status changes to Partial or Paid.

## Reading status colours
Grey = new, blue = in production, amber = trial/alteration or due soon, red = overdue or balance due, green = ready/paid/delivered.

## Notifications
Overdue, due soon, balance due at Ready, trials waiting. They update automatically when orders change.

## Tips
- Use search on Orders and Customers (name, phone, order number) and the stage filter.
- Never share your password. Sign out on shared devices.
- Wrong entry? Payments and status history cannot be edited; tell your manager, who can record a correcting entry.
