# Vision

## The problem

India has tens of millions of micro and small businesses. Most have no accountant and no lawyer. They hear about GST, Udyam, trade licence or data privacy from neighbours, agents or WhatsApp forwards, and they get it wrong: they miss a return and pay a late fee, pay an agent for a free registration, or never register and lose access to bank credit. The law is not hidden, but it is written for professionals, on portals built for professionals.

## The promise

> Tell us about your business once. We tell you what to do, when, and how, in your language, one small step at a time.

## Who it is for

| Segment | Examples | Typical needs |
|---|---|---|
| Shop owners | Kirana, garment, hardware, mobile shop | GST threshold, composition scheme, shops act, trade licence |
| Small vendors | Street food, vegetable, tea stall, home kitchen | FSSAI, Udyam, local permissions, UPI income tracking |
| Service providers | Salon, repair, tuition, electrician, photographer | GST for services, presumptive tax (44ADA), Udyam |
| Tailors and boutiques | Alteration shops, boutiques, bridal studios | All of the above plus customer measurement data, staff, GST on job work |

## Principles

1. **Chat first, never a form wall.** Six questions to onboard. After that, the trader receives posts and taps buttons.
2. **Their language.** English, Hindi and Kannada first. Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali next. Short sentences, no jargon, the legal term in brackets only when they will meet it on a portal.
3. **One post, one action.** Each post covers one obligation with a due date, why it matters, and at most five steps.
4. **Honest about limits.** We give guidance, not advice. Every post says so and points to the official portal. We never file on the trader's behalf in this project.
5. **Never sell fear or agents.** No upsell of "compliance packages". Where something is free (Udyam), we say so loudly.
6. **Rules are data.** Every rule has a source, a review status and a last-checked date. A CA can review the catalogue without reading code.
7. **Private by default.** The trader's turnover and licence details are theirs. No sharing, no ads.

## Scope

**In (this project):** profile, rule catalogue, reminders, step-by-step guides, "Done / Remind me / Help me" tracking, a document locker for certificates, channel delivery over WhatsApp and in-app.

**Out for now:** filing returns for the trader, GSP/API integration with the GST portal, tax computation, advice on tax planning, legal drafting. These need licences or professional liability and come later through partner CAs.

## Success measures

- Share of traders who complete onboarding in under 3 minutes
- Share of reminders marked Done before the due date
- Late fees avoided (self-reported, then measured via partner CAs)
- Rules reviewed by a CA within 30 days of any notification that changes them
